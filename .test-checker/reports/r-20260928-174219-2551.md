### sgtlearn-ensemble-random-sgforest-regressor-py--randomsgforestregressor-check-x-y

Logical mistakes: none found. The method is a thin validation wrapper for regression training data. Its settings fit the forest's stated capabilities. Dense input only. X is coerced to float64 and NaN is allowed in X, because the underlying SGT trees handle missing values. Targets must be numeric. Multi-output (2-D) targets are allowed, as a multi-output regressor needs.
Robustness omissions: none found in the method itself. Minor notes: (1) the finiteness relaxation applies only to X. y must still be fully finite (no NaN or inf), which is correct for regression targets. (2) y is not forced to float64. Integer targets keep their integer dtype and only object-dtype targets are converted to float64, so callers must not assume y comes back as float64. (3) The `ensure_all_finite` keyword needs a recent scikit-learn (>= 1.6). On older versions the call fails with a TypeError. This is an environment constraint, not a logic defect.
